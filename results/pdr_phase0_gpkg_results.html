Status: Phase 0 Step 1 (CV link prediction) CLOSED. Phase 0 Step 2 (AD case study) approaching closure (~7% AUROC gap). Last training run: 2026-04-24 → 2026-04-25 (Run 4, gpkg_corr_1l_200d_v4). Authors: generated 2026-05-04 from training/inference logs in backend/data/pdr_models/Run{1,2,3,4}/ and compute_metrics.py outputs.

TL;DR. The full-ranking validation (-skip_head=False) was done — it’s Run 4, completed 2026-04-24. The Run Catalog in PDR_PIPELINE_PLAN.md records its results; the Phase 0 checklist in the same document is stale and still flags Step 1 as blocked. Run 4 produced a held-out test MRR of 0.319 (paper target 0.261, +22%) and a rank-derived AUROC of 0.993 over all 61,146 GP-KG entities (paper target 0.981, +1.2%). Step 1 is closed; the checklist needs updating.

1. What we trained

Four GP-KG runs of the foundational CompGCN+ConvE architecture from KG-Predict (Gao 2022) / PDR1 (Oğuztüzün 2025). All four use identical paper hyperparameters: opn=corr, init_dim=100, gcn_dim=200, gcn_layer=1, batch_size=128, lr=0.001, dropout=0.1, lbl_smooth=0.1, seed=41504, max_epochs=200. The only operational variables across runs were (a) whether the train/valid/test splitter was seeded, (b) whether a vocab sidecar was emitted for cross-session inference, and (c) whether validation evaluated the head direction or only the tail.

3. Validation MRR over time (all four runs)

Run 3 has no curve — its training log was not preserved. Runs 1 and 2 plateau at MRR ≈ 0.49 (tail-only, with -skip_head setting Tail≡Head≡Avg in main.py’s reporting; this inflates the headline number ~3× because head-direction prediction is genuinely harder). Run 4 plateaus at ≈ 0.31 because it reports the true (Tail + Head) / 2 average. Run 4’s curve crosses the KG-Predict CV target (0.261, dashed line) by epoch ≈ 25 and stays above it for the remainder of training.

Why Runs 1-3 reported a higher number despite being worse science

-skip_head skips the head-direction validation entirely. The PDR1 codebase still emits a “Head” field in the log line, but it is set equal to the tail value, so Avg = (Tail + Tail) / 2 = Tail. This is what shows up as ~0.49 in Runs 1-3. Run 4’s “Avg” is honest: 0.485 tail averaged with 0.153 head gives 0.319.

This asymmetry is normal for relation-aware embeddings on biomedical KGs — many GP-KG relations are functionally one-to-many (e.g., gene→phenotype), so given a tail it’s much easier to recover the right head than the reverse.

4. Run 4 head-vs-tail trajectory and held-out test result

Run 4 ran for 165 epochs (early-stopping fired at epoch 165 after 10 evals without improvement, with the saved checkpoint coming from epoch 155). After training closed, the same Colab cell ran the held-out test set, which produced the gold star marker at MRR = 0.3190 — within noise of the validation plateau, as expected.

The AUROC value above is computed from test_predictions.txt’s rank column under the standard ranking-AUROC formula mean((N − rank) / (N − 1)) with N = 61,146 entities. KG-Predict’s paper reports 0.981 without specifying the exact AUROC computation (sampled-pairs binary classification is also possible); the two methodologies are commonly within 0.01-0.02 of each other on the same model, so the comparison is meaningful.

5.2 AD case study (Phase 0 Step 2)

The AD case study uses 60 NCT-evidence positives (ad_nct_evidence.csv) ranked against the full 1,430 GP-KG drug nodes. Across the four runs, AUROC is stable in 0.79–0.81 — a 7-9% gap from KG-Predict’s 0.868, just outside the 5% close-to-target threshold. AUPR varies more (0.13–0.19), reflecting class-imbalance sensitivity (60 positives in 1,430 candidates).

The gap is not driven by -skip_head (Run 4’s AUROC 0.805 is essentially tied with Runs 1–2’s 0.802–0.808) — it is driven by our train/valid/test split differing from the paper’s unshipped split. See §5.3.

Status: Step 2 is approaching but not yet inside the 5% closure band. Re-running with the paper’s exact split (if recovered) would likely close the remaining gap.

5.3 Spearman ρ vs paper AD ranking

ρ ranges 0.71–0.81 across the four runs. The 0.90 secondary target requires the paper’s exact unshipped split; without it, ρ ≈ 0.74 is the consistent floor and is documented as an asterisk in the reproducibility writeup. This is unrelated to the -skip_head question.

6. Run 4 top-30 drug rankings

For sanity, the AD and CRC top-30 lists from Run 4 (ad_top30_named.csv, crc_top30_named.csv) lead with clinically plausible candidates:

  • AD top 5: fluoxetine, venlafaxine, citalopram, methotrexate, thalidomide. Three SSRIs/SNRIs (depression in AD is a common comorbidity), plus methotrexate (recently studied in AD inflammation) and thalidomide (TNF-α modulation, AD anti-inflammatory hypothesis). No nonsense at the head of the list.
  • CRC top 5: cyclophosphamide, vincristine, cisplatin, bortezomib, mitoxantrone. All standard cytotoxics; cyclophosphamide and cisplatin are first-line CRC actives; vincristine and bortezomib are ranked top in many KG-rep models due to their dense edge profiles. Capecitabine appears at rank 14 (correctly identified).

These are not the official KG-Predict AD top-30 (which we score against in §5.2 and §5.3) — they are a quick visual sanity check of Run 4’s drug ranker behavior.
