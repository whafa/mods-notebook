cBioPortal for MODS, October 2026

Issue: #658 · Builds on: #590 (cohort funnel), #327 (external cohort track) Script: backend/scripts/cohorts/cbioportal_inventory.py Data: the public API, portal v7.1.2, database 3.0.0, gene table hgnc_v7_2025.10.7. The script fetched everything between 08:10 and 08:18 PDT on 2026-10-02 (613 requests). An exploratory pass from 07:49 gave the same counts. Web sources were read the same day.

What this is for. The cohort funnel (#590) used one slice of cBioPortal to answer one question: how many public patients have tumours like hers. This is the whole-site look. It covers what the portal holds, the tools and services around it, what the terms allow, and which pieces serve MODS. It builds nothing except the read-only script behind the numbers.

Summary

  • What cBioPortal is, for us. The largest free, uniform window onto clinical cancer genomics. It offers:

    • 548 public studies behind one REST API with no login;
    • a weekly dump of the whole database;
    • open services around it: Genome Nexus for variant annotation, OncoTree for cancer types, Cancer Hotspots.

    It is a source of evidence about patients and models like her. It is not a drug-response source: its one drug-response dataset is a 2019 copy of GDSC. It is not a knowledge-graph source. Beyond overall survival, only MSK-CHORD’s treatment timelines carry outcomes.

  • For her disease it holds 62 studies with colorectal samples, nearly twice the funnel’s 34. They include MSK-IMPACT 50K, MSK-MET, NCI’s patient-derived models, and several organoid and cell-line collections (§1.2).

  • What the API already showed about her (§5):

    • Variants. Her APC frameshift is a recurrent hotspot, in 1.9–2.9% of colorectal patients in four cohorts. Her TGFBR2, MGA and NTRK2 variants appear at their residues in none of about 9,000 patients. Getting that right needed genomic coordinates, because protein strings mislead across sources.
    • Liver groups. Joining MSK-MET to MSK-CHORD gives an independent check on the Neighbors liver groups: 90% agreement, with “no liver” confirmed for 38 of 40. It also shows that disease like hers, nodal without liver, is three public patients.
    • Models. Six BRAF V600E, MSS colorectal patient-derived models are orderable from NCI, with exome and RNA-seq on the portal.

  • Recommendations, in order (§6):

    1. a shared client with read-only MCP tools;
    2. the MSK-MET cross-check for #614 (2026-10-05: #614 was decided as D-Q4-13, and the approved plan leaves the MSK-MET line out);
    3. per-variant cohort context in the Genomic Browser and the Options Ledger;
    4. Genome Nexus: Pfam domains on the lollipop now, the variant resolver later;
    5. a proxy-model shortlist on #327.

    GENIE is Richard’s call.

  • Not now:

    • a self-hosted portal holding her data;
    • cohort-derived KG edges;
    • TCGA as her expression reference. It failed in June, for reasons that still apply.

  • Found in passing:

    • OncoKB’s terms. They forbid AI/ML training. How MODS uses OncoKB is a licence decision recorded on the project’s tracker (#660).
      • OncoKB approved the project’s account on 2026-05-18 (#680).
    • A false answer without a token. OncoKB’s no-token API says there are no approved treatments for BRAF V600E colon cancer. MODS’s connectors use the tokened API, and any new path must too.

1.2 Colorectal cancer across the whole portal

The funnel searched the bowel subtree of OncoTree plus the mixed studies that carry treatment data, 34 studies in all. Counting colorectal samples in every study finds 62 studies and 44,986 colorectal samples. A sample counts when its OncoTree code is one of the seven the funnel uses (COAD, READ, COADREAD, MACR, SRCCR, CMC, CAIS). Three studies have no OncoTree field; for those, the cancer-type text or the study’s own cancer type decides. No other colorectal code is in use: the remaining bowel codes are appendiceal, anal, small-bowel, neuroendocrine or adenoma.

Some of the hits are not tumour data:

  • Blood. The three clonal-haematopoiesis studies hold 6,451 of the colorectal samples, almost all in msk_ch_2023 and msk_ch_2020. Their mutations were called in blood, and the cancer type is the patient’s. Never count them as tumour samples. Without them the total is 38,535.
  • Polyps. crc_hta11_htan_2021 holds 61 precancerous polyps.

1.3 What the one drug-response dataset is

CCLE 2019’s three drug-response profiles cover 266 compounds, not the two dozen of the original CCLE drug panel. Every compound links to cancerrxgene.org, and the curve fits cite Vis et al. 2016, the GDSC fitting method. So it is a pre-2019 snapshot of the Sanger GDSC screens, mapped onto CCLE lines. It includes dabrafenib, PLX-4720, trametinib, cetuximab, 5-FU and SN-38. 55 of the portal’s 84 colorectal lines have IC50 rows, and so do 9 of its 10 BRAF V600E colorectal lines (all but COLO201).

For the Q4 proxy work (D16: PRISM first, GDSC2 as replication) it is a convenience copy, not a source. The primary releases are newer, and its licence is ambiguous (§4).

Three things follow for MODS:

  • Pin and record. Every pull should store the portal version, the fetch window and the source study, as the funnel does. Nothing in the data says when it changed.
  • The dump beats paging for anything broad. One 176 MB mutation file answers “how often is this variant seen, anywhere” for the whole portal, and it reaches crc_msk_2026, which has no tarball.
  • Nothing of hers goes to the hosted AI services. Their terms log identity and content.

3.1 Maple’s own client and tools (built 2026-10-02, #667)

Recommendation 1 (§6) is built.

  • One client. backend/connectors/cbioportal_client.py is shared by the funnel, the inventory and the tools. It has a process-wide 0.6 s limiter, retries that honour Retry-After, typed errors and an atomic disk cache. Its cache key is #590’s, so old caches replay unchanged.
  • Three read-only MCP tools in mods_server.py, built on connectors/cbioportal_queries.py:
    • mods_cbioportal_studies: find studies, with data types and stated licence;
    • mods_cbioportal_variant_frequency: per-study patient counts for a variant or residue. It matches the exact string and the GRCh37 coordinate, reports panel coverage, and refuses blood studies;
    • mods_cbioportal_cohort_counts: attribute values and treatment-agent patients under an OncoTree filter. Every answer carries the portal version and the fetch window. Tool answers are cached for 7 days, in a cache separate from the scripts’.
  • Proof the switch changed nothing. With the hash seed pinned, both scripts replay their caches offline to byte-identical JSON under the old and new clients. Their JSON is now written with sorted keys, so a replay is byte-identical whatever the seed.

4. Services around the portal, and the terms

4.1 Genome Nexus: variant annotation

MSK’s annotation service, and the one cBioPortal itself uses. One call returns, per variant:

  • VEP consequences and a canonical transcript;
  • SIFT, PolyPhen and AlphaMissense;
  • Cancer Hotspots and Mutation Assessor;
  • ClinVar, SignalDB (MSK germline frequencies) and myvariant.info;
  • OncoKB, only when a token is passed.

The software is MIT; the data carry their sources’ licences.

InstanceBuildServerHotspotsClinVar
www.genomenexus.org (and v1, which the portal uses)GRCh372.2.3, VEP 116v32026-02-08
grch38.genomenexus.orgGRCh381.4.1, lagging on purposev22025-01-06

Live probes with the textbook BRAF V600E:

  • GRCh37 works. The canonical transcript is ENST00000288602.6, c.1799T>A, p.V600E. The response carries the V600 hotspot (897 tumours), Mutation Assessor “high”, and AlphaMissense 0.99.
  • GRCh38 misnames it by default. It picks ENST00000288602.11 and returns p.V640E, with no hotspots. The MSK isoform override restores V600E, but hotspots stay empty. Her Caris VCFs are GRCh38, so her calls should be annotated on GRCh37: lifted over, or queried by transcript HGVS-c.
  • It accepts more than coordinates. BRAF:p.V600E (with a colon) and ENST00000288602:c.1799T>A both resolve. BRAF p.V600E (with a space) fails silently.
  • Failures return HTTP 200. A record can come back successfully_annotated: false with no error text. OncoKB is dropped without notice when no token is passed. The rsID endpoint annotates every ALT allele at the site. A client has to check each record.
  • Some of the payload is not licence-clean. SignalDB carries OncoKB-style terms, academic only with no “patient services”, and myvariant.info passes COSMIC through. Request fields deliberately.

Two uses for MODS fall out of this:

  • The lollipop can get Pfam domains with two unauthenticated calls. These are GET /ensembl/canonical-transcript/hgnc/{gene} and POST /pfam/domain, the calls cBioPortal’s own mutation mapper makes. This is #173 item 7, with no need to adopt the AGPL component.
  • It can canonicalize her variants for cohort joins (§5.1). It can also settle the Phase 8.5 resolver’s misses: an HGVS-p-only missense resolves through GENE:p.X. A novel indel annotates from its coordinates, but it still has no ClinGen CAID.

4.2 OncoTree: the cancer-type vocabulary

  • Versions and licence. CC BY 4.0. The latest stable release is oncotree_2025_10_03, with 897 nodes; the portal’s 897 cancer types are this tree. Each node carries NCIt and UMLS cross-references.

  • The crosswalk to MONDO lives on Mondo’s side, as xrefs:

    OncoTreeMondo
    COADREADMONDO:0005008, colorectal adenocarcinoma
    COADMONDO:0002271, colon adenocarcinoma
    READMONDO:0002169, rectum adenocarcinoma

  • None of them is MODS’s colorectal anchor. The KG anchors colorectal cancer at MONDO:0005575, which carries no OncoTree xref. Which Mondo node a cohort lands on is a vocabulary decision, so it is flagged here, not made (CLAUDE.md hard rule).

  • OncoTree’s own mapping file has no MONDO column and lags the latest release by 52 codes.

4.3 Cancer Hotspots

v3 adds 164 hotspots (Bandlamudi et al., Cancer Cell 2026), mined from the MSK-IMPACT 50K cohort above. Data are ODbL and the software AGPL; there is a public API. Hotspots are single residues and in-frame indels, so a frameshift like her APC variant is out of scope by design; §5.1 is the question for those.

4.4 OncoKB, inside and outside the portal

  • On the public portal, OncoKB cards come through the portal’s own token. Its proxy demands a header in which the caller attests that it is not fetching OncoKB data programmatically, so code must never use it.
  • The no-token public API gives a false answer for her. For BRAF V600E in colon adenocarcinoma it returns “There are no FDA-approved or NCCN-compendium listed treatments specifically for patients with BRAF V600E mutant colon adenocarcinoma.” It says the same for melanoma, so this comes from the stripped data, not from curation. Anything that calls that API directly would pass the sentence on. (The Genome Nexus pipeline just omits OncoKB without a token.)
    • MODS’s connectors call the tokened API and refuse to run without a token (oncokb_annotate_connector.py: DEFAULT_API_BASE, download()). Any new path must keep that property.
  • The terms forbid training on it. The licensing FAQ says OncoKB “cannot be used to train AI/ML models whether for academic or commercial purposes”; benchmarking needs explicit permission. “Use for patient services” needs a licence agreement.
    • maureen_focus_v1 includes OncoKB; how MODS uses it is a licence decision recorded on the project’s tracker (#660).
    • OncoKB approved the project’s account in May 2026 (#680).
    • The stakes are small. OncoKB is 483 of about 8.0 million relations in that composition. 22 of them concern her variants, and CIViC duplicates 9 of BRAF V600E’s 16. (2026-10-05: #744’s re-annotation has since grown the OncoKB source to 7,217 relations.)

5. Three things the API can already do for her

These are demonstrations, not analyses. Each one is a pattern that a MODS integration would make routine.

  • Her APC frameshift is a recurrent hotspot, the most common change at its residue in every cohort. Her BRAF V600E is common, though cohorts differ several-fold. DFCI’s cohort is BRAF- and MSI-rich: by the funnel’s rule only 37 of its 111 V600E patients are MSS.
  • Her TGFBR2, MGA and NTRK2 variants appear at their residues in none of about 9,000 patients. The genes themselves are often mutated (403, 415 and 148 patients in crc_msk_2026). The panels cover them, except MGA on IMPACT341 (202 samples). For the NTRK2 V717M VUS, no recurrence is weak evidence against a hotspot role, not a verdict.
  • Protein strings are not a safe join key. The first pass counted her APC variant as absent, because cBioPortal gives T1556Nfs*3 (a duplication in a run of As) a proteinPosStart of 1554, not 1556. And TGFBR2 is numbered on different isoforms in different studies: residue 522 reads D in the MSK data and R in TCGA’s. The script matches on the exact protein-change string and checks it against the GRCh37 genomic coordinate. For APC and BRAF the two agree exactly in all four cohorts. Any variant-level join in MODS should go through genomic coordinates and one canonical annotation (§4, Genome Nexus).

5.2 Joining studies on MSK-IMPACT IDs: liver and distant nodes

MSK studies share patient IDs, so one study can annotate another. MSK-MET (about 25,000 patients) records, per organ, whether a distant metastasis was diagnosed, taken from pathology reports and billing codes (Nguyen et al. 2022). That includes distant lymph nodes. The funnel had no such field: MSK-CHORD’s lymph-node flag mixes regional and distant nodes, so the funnel could not pick out nodal disease like hers.   • “Unspecified” is common. 17 of the 49 patients with no liver flag carry only the unspecified-site flag, so absence of a named site is weak evidence.

10OncoKB through the portal’s proxy, or the no-token API.———Never. One is attested against, and the other gives a false answer for her (§4.4).

The ranking follows two lessons from this repo. A tested shared client comes before features, because the funnel and this spike both had to rebuild the same plumbing. And population data serves decisions and checks, not the model, until the model beats its baselines (Q4 roadmap §3.2).

Why build tools rather than adopt the official MCP server or BioMCP:

  • The official server needs a Google sign-in and logs every query against the account.
  • Both serve Claude sessions only. Maple’s own backend and agents can’t call them.
  • Neither shares the funnel’s cache, pacing or fetch-window records.

BioMCP is still a reasonable stopgap for ad-hoc public questions in a session.

F3 as built (2026-10-04)

The Genomic Browser’s Table tab now has a CRC cohorts column: patients carrying the variant, out of patients sequenced over the gene, across every public colorectal study on the portal.

  • How it is built. scripts/cohorts/build_variant_context.py writes backend/data/cohorts/cbioportal/variant_context.json (gitignored). GET /api/mods/vcf/cohort-frequency serves it. The rules are in mods_variant_context_service.py, and the pooled count is pooled_variant_frequency in connectors/cbioportal_queries.py. A full build takes about four minutes at the 0.6 s pace.
  • Which calls. Somatic calls from the two colorectal specimens: tiered Pathogenic to VUS, VAF ≥ 0.05, and absent from the separate melanoma specimen. A call that two independent cancers share is inherited, so it is left out. Without that second specimen the build refuses.
  • The join. Her GRCh38 calls are lifted to GRCh37 locally (pyliftover, UCSC chain), so no coordinate of hers is sent anywhere. The portal is asked for gene-level calls only. The exact-match string is the one the portal records at her coordinate (T1556Nfs*3, where Caris writes T1556fs).
  • Which studies. 28 studies are typed colorectal, typed bowel, or sit under colorectal. A bowel-typed study without OncoTree codes is skipped, and so are the msk_ch_* studies. MSK-IMPACT, TCGA and CPTAC IDs count once across studies; any other ID is local to its study.
  • Check against §5.1. Restricted to the four cohorts, the build reproduces §5.1 exactly: APC 195 / 11 / 18 / 23 and BRAF 537 / 48 / 111 / 23. String and coordinate agree in all four (test_live_survey_counts_reproduce, opt-in).
  • A disagreement the column shows rather than hides. Over all 28 studies, crc_msk_2017 and coadread_mskcc anchor the same A-run insertion one base later (5:112175952:-:A). There the string finds 30 patients and the coordinate finds 5. The cell carries a ⚠, and the tooltip gives the per-study counts.
  • Not done: the Options Ledger. A Ledger fact cannot cite this until the Ledger schema lands (#565, #613).

8. Limits

  • Counts are as of 2026-10-02, from portal v7.1.2. Studies are reloaded in place, and the API is beta.
  • MSK-MET’s site flags come from billing codes and pathology reports (Nguyen et al. 2022), which the authors say miss some events. “Unspecified” is common.
  • The MSS rule is the funnel’s. Group sizes inherit its definitions, including its judgement calls.
  • Untested: loading a one-patient study into a self-hosted instance; embedding react-mutation-mapper in Maple; whether the API is throttled at the infrastructure level.
  • Unverified: the MSS status of CL34 and SNUC5; the current DepMap and HCMI terms; whether GENIE BPC CRC is browsable on the GENIE portal as well as on Synapse (de Bruijn et al. 2023 describes BPC data in cBioPortal).
  • The transcript problem is general. Any protein-level comparison across sources can mislead. §5.1 found two cases in five variants.

Reproduce

The numbers above measure the portal on 2026-10-02, and it reloads weekly. To replay this measurement, point the script at its snapshot in the main checkout:

python backend/scripts/cohorts/cbioportal_inventory.py --offline --cache-dir C:/Maple/backend/data/cohorts/cbioportal/snapshots/2026-10-02_658/http_cache
python backend/scripts/cohorts/cbioportal_inventory.py --json backend/data/cohorts/cbioportal/inventory.json
  • The snapshot holds the run’s 613 cached responses and its output, inventory_2026-10-02.json. It is host-only and gitignored, because MSK-CHORD and MSK-MET are CC BY-NC-ND. An offline replay on 2026-10-04 sent no request and reproduced that output exactly.
  • A fresh measurement (the second command) sends about 650 requests, one at a time with a 0.6 s pause, and caches them under backend/data/cohorts/cbioportal/http_cache/ in the checkout it runs in. Copy that cache into a new dated snapshot before the session ends.
  • Each step prints its own fetch window in PDT.
